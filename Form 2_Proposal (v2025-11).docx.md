| TAR UMT Campus Shuttle Management System : Real-Time Seat Booking, QR Boarding & Fleet Monitoring Module |
| **ABSTRACT** |
| Campus bus services at TAR UMT suffer from inefficient manual seat allocation, high no-show rates, and the absence of real-time visibility for students and administrators. This project proposes a full-stack web-based Campus Shuttle Management System enabling students to book seats digitally, receive dynamic anti-fraud QR boarding passes, and join automated waitlists. Drivers use a dedicated portal to scan QR codes and control trip lifecycle status, while administrators manage the full fleet through a real-time dashboard with analytics. Built with Next.js 16, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL, Socket.io, and JWT authentication, the system implements a credit-based penalty mechanism to discourage no-shows. The expected outcome is a secure, scalable campus transport platform that reduces seat wastage, improves operational transparency, and enhances the commuter experience for the university community. |
| **PROBLEM** |
| Campus shuttle services at Malaysian universities, including TAR UMT, remain largely manual or semi-digital, relying on physical queues or static timetable boards. This creates several well-documented operational problems: <br><br> **1. Seat Wastage Due to No-Shows:** Students who informally reserve seats often fail to board without notice, leaving seats empty and denying transport to waiting students. Studies on campus transport in Southeast Asia report that no-show rates in informal booking schemes can exceed 20–30% of total seat capacity (Ismail et al., 2020). <br><br> **2. Lack of Real-Time Visibility:** Neither students nor administrators can observe live seat availability or trip status, leading to overcrowding at stops and missed buses. <br><br> **3. Absence of Accountability Mechanisms:** Without a digital identity-linked booking system, there is no enforceable penalty for repeated no-shows and no audit trail for disputes. <br><br> **4. Operational Inefficiency for Administrators:** Fleet management and driver assignments handled via spreadsheets make it difficult to respond to disruptions such as bus breakdowns or route changes in a timely manner. <br><br> These issues align with the broader challenge of digitising university transport infrastructure in Malaysia, as highlighted by the Ministry of Higher Education's Smart Campus Blueprint (MOHE, 2022). The absence of an integrated, real-time digital solution creates compounding inefficiencies affecting thousands of students daily. <br><br> **References:** <br> Ismail, A., Rahman, M. F. A., & Hamid, N. A. (2020). Campus bus service management and student satisfaction: A review of Malaysian public universities. *Journal of Transport and Land Use*, 13(1), 541–558. https://doi.org/10.5198/jtlu.2020.1588 <br> Ministry of Higher Education Malaysia (MOHE). (2022). *Smart campus blueprint 2022–2025*. Putrajaya: MOHE. |
| **SOLUTION** |
| The proposed solution is a web-based Campus Shuttle Management System with three role-specific portals — Student, Driver, and Admin — integrated via a real-time Socket.io service and backed by a Prisma/PostgreSQL database. <br><br> **Key Capabilities:** <br><br> - **Student Portal:** Students search routes, select travel dates and journey segments, view real-time seat availability, and confirm bookings through a guided wizard (Route → Date → Segment → Seat). A dynamic anti-fraud QR boarding pass using a 60-second expiring JWT (auto-refreshed every 45 seconds) is issued to prevent screenshot fraud. An automated waitlist promotes students when cancellations occur. A credit score (default 100) deducts 15 points per no-show and restricts booking access below a threshold. Students may submit written penalty appeals. <br><br> - **Driver Portal:** Drivers view assigned trips, scan or paste student QR tokens for check-in verification, and control trip lifecycle (Boarding → Departed → Arrived). A live seat matrix shows real-time occupancy. <br><br> - **Admin Portal:** Full fleet management (CRUD for buses, routes, trips, and driver accounts) with cascade safety (e.g., trip cancellation on bus retirement). Real-time seat grids with simulated IoT sensor health signals (OK / OFFLINE / ERROR). Historical analytics dashboard using Recharts covering ridership, route demand, and fleet utilisation. Penalty and appeal review with admin commenting. <br><br> - **Automated Cron Jobs:** A standalone Node.js service runs 1-minute cron jobs to auto-detect no-shows after boarding deadlines, deduct credit points, issue penalties, and push real-time notifications to all parties. <br><br> **Critical Functions:** <br> 1. Seat booking with real-time availability enforcement and race-condition-safe duplicate prevention (Prisma `$transaction`) <br> 2. Dynamic anti-fraud QR boarding pass generation and 60-second token verification <br> 3. Waitlist auto-promotion upon booking cancellation <br> 4. Automated no-show detection, penalty issuance, and credit score deduction via cron job <br> 5. Trip lifecycle management (scheduling, delay, cancellation with cascade to bookings and notifications) <br> 6. Real-time seat occupancy broadcast via Socket.io to all subscribed clients <br> 7. Admin fleet management with cascade safety (trip cancellation on bus retirement or maintenance) <br> 8. Penalty appeal submission and admin adjudication workflow <br> 9. Simulated IoT seat sensor health monitoring in the admin dashboard |

| **TARGET MARKET** |
| **Primary Users:** <br><br> - **Students (TAR UMT):** The largest user group. Students register accounts, search and book bus seats, use QR boarding passes, join waitlists, and receive real-time notifications for confirmations, delays, cancellations, and penalties. They benefit from transparent availability, no-queue digital boarding, and a fair credit-based system. <br><br> - **Bus Drivers (TAR UMT Fleet):** Driver accounts are created by admins. Drivers use the portal daily to view assigned trips, scan student QR codes for check-in, and update trip status. The portal replaces manual headcounting and paper-based boarding records. <br><br> **Secondary / Administrative Users:** <br><br> - **Campus Transport Administrators (TAR UMT Operations):** Admins manage the entire fleet, configure routes and schedules, assign drivers, monitor real-time occupancy, review historical analytics, and adjudicate student penalty appeals. Full operational oversight is provided through the admin dashboard. <br><br> **Indirect Stakeholders:** <br><br> - **University Management:** Benefits from data-driven ridership and fleet utilisation analytics for budget planning and service improvement decisions. <br><br> - **Other Malaysian Public Universities:** The generalised architecture can be adapted for similar campus shuttle services at other institutions, supporting the national Smart Campus initiative. |
| **COMPETITION / CONTRIBUTION** |
| **Existing Solutions & Their Gaps:** <br><br> - **Generic Ride-Hailing Apps (Grab, MyCar):** Not designed for fixed-route campus shuttles; do not support pre-allocated seat matrices, a credit-penalty system, or driver-side trip lifecycle management. <br> - **University Mobile Apps (e.g., UM MyUniLife, UTM apps):** Offer timetable viewing but typically do not provide seat-level booking, real-time occupancy updates, or anti-fraud QR boarding. Most lack automated no-show enforcement. <br> - **Static Booking Forms / WhatsApp Groups:** Common informal solutions at Malaysian universities; have no race-condition safety, no audit trail, no real-time updates, and rely entirely on manual administration. <br><br> **Unique Differentiators of This Project:** <br><br> 1. **Anti-Fraud Dynamic QR Boarding:** 60-second expiring JWT QR codes refreshed every 45 seconds prevent screenshot sharing — a feature absent from all identified campus transport apps. <br> 2. **Credit Score & Penalty-Appeal Lifecycle:** A fully automated, end-to-end no-show accountability system with student-initiated appeals and admin adjudication — not found in existing campus transport tools. <br> 3. **Decoupled Real-Time Architecture:** The Socket.io service is architecturally isolated from the Next.js app, enabling independent scaling and zero coupling between the booking API and real-time broadcast layer. <br> 4. **Simulated IoT Seat Sensor Monitoring:** Per-seat device health signals (OK / OFFLINE / ERROR) in the admin dashboard provide a proof-of-concept for future IoT hardware integration. <br> 5. **Race-Condition-Safe Concurrent Booking:** Duplicate booking prevention inside Prisma `$transaction` blocks ensures correctness under simultaneous concurrent requests — a production-grade concern overlooked in academic prototypes. <br><br> **Contribution to Stakeholders & Community:** <br><br> - Reduces seat wastage and improves equitable transport access for students. <br> - Reduces administrative burden for operations staff through full automation of no-show detection and penalty workflows. <br> - Provides university management with analytics for data-driven fleet planning. <br> - Serves as a reference implementation supporting the Malaysian government's Smart Campus digitalisation agenda. |
